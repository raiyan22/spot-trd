# How realistic is $300 -> $350? (2026-10-02)

Frozen v2, BTC spot only, $300 start, **no contributions**, risk per trade as per v2's schedule (1% at $300), no trade forced. All progress is counted on **realized** equity (initial + closed-trade P&L). Scenarios were fixed before the first run: base, conservative (half the average edge removed), harsh fills, gap stress, and no edge. Nothing was tuned.

## 1. The evidence base

- v2 on $300 over 8.6 years (2018-03-04 to 2026-10-01): 51 trades = **5.9 trades a year**, win rate 41%, average +0.62R, average trade +0.47% of equity, median trade -0.34%.
- Profit per year at $300: base **$+8.89**, harsh $+7.10, gap stress $+4.13; 2023-onward (contaminated, consistency only) $+7.13.
- The audit found that three trades produced 78% of the profit, so the average hides a very lumpy process: most trades are small losses, a few trend trades pay for them.

## 2. Straight arithmetic: years of *average* profit needed for +$50

| Scenario | Average profit per year at $300 | Years to +$50 at that average |
|---|---|---|
| Base | $+8.89 | 5.6 years |
| Harsh fills | $+7.10 | 7.0 years |
| Gap stress | $+4.13 | 12.1 years |
| Conservative (half the edge) | $+4.45 | 11.2 years |
| No edge | $0 (before the fee drag already in the numbers) | never |

The average trade is only known to within a wide band: bootstrap 95% interval for the mean trade -0.07% to +1.09% of equity (that is $-1.2 to $+19.5 a year at $300). The lower end is at or below zero.

## 6. Verdict

- **Base case:** the average-profit arithmetic says about 5.6 years. The simulation agrees that the *typical* path takes years: median 4.5 years among paths that get there within 10 years, with 22% reaching $350 by year 3, 45% by year 5 and 80% by year 10.
- **Conservative (half the edge):** 27% by year 5, 53% by year 10.
- **Harsh fills:** 36% by year 5, 72% by year 10.
- **No edge:** 28% reach $350 by year 10 purely by luck; the same simulation with no edge also leaves a large share below $300 for long stretches.
- **Historically**, the one real path (start 2018-03) reached $350 realized after 3.0 years and never fell below $300, because it started just before two good years. That was a favourable start: across all start dates only 31% had reached $350 by the end of the data, marked equity spent a median 12% of days below $300 (worst start 100%) and the longest stretch below $300 reached 3.6 years. Separately, the equity curve went 1303 days (3.6 years) without a new high.
- There is **no honest deadline**. If the strategy's true edge is close to what history shows, $350 is a multi-year outcome; if the edge is half of what it appears, it is a decade-long one; if the edge is illusory, there is no reason to expect $350 at all. A paper test cannot tell these apart quickly: telling the observed average from zero needs about 100 or more trades, about 19 years at this rate.
- Risk is not increased at any milestone, and none of this changes v2. The earlier higher-risk study already showed that raising risk to speed this up fails on probability of loss, not just on drawdown.

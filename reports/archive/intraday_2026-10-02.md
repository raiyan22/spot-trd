## 1. Look-ahead verification (before results are read)

- Indicators at a candle equal their value computed from candles up to it only: **PASS**.
- Perturbation: after 4 random cut dates every later candle was replaced by different prices; the list of trades that exit by the cut (entry time, exit time, return) is identical for A, B and C: **PASS**.
- Leak-direction check as pre-registered (acting on a candle's signal at that same candle's open), mean net return per trade, correct vs leaked: A -0.166% vs +0.734%, B -0.253% vs +0.269%, C -0.353% vs -1.782%: **does not pass for every strategy**. Non-gating after Amendment 1: for a mean-reversion entry, buying at the signal candle's open is a worse price, so this check cannot show power for C.
- Mutation test (Amendment 1): a deliberately look-ahead version of each strategy (needs the close 24 candles later to be higher) was run through the same perturbation check at 8 cut dates (midnight after days on which the mutant traded); leak detected on A 3 of 8 cuts, B 4 of 8 cuts, C 6 of 8 cuts: **PASS (harness can detect a leak)**.
- Daily gates use the previous completed day only (a gate that is True only on day D first applies at 2022-03-12, D+1): **PASS**.
- Schedule: no entry after the 20:00 open, none held overnight, at most one entry a day (violations: 0): **PASS**.

All-clear rule (perturbation passes and the mutation test shows the harness can detect a leak; see `intraday_preregistration_amendment_1_2026-10-02.md`): **YES**.

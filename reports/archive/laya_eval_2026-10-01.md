# Laya evaluation (2026-10-01)

Question: does Laya (convaiinnovations/laya, zero-shot) improve decisions beyond the rules?
Target: is BTC higher 10 days later? Selected and calibrated on 2018-2022; judged on 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-20 (1359 days).

## Ranking skill on unseen 2023+ days (AUC 0.5 = coin flip)

| Signal | AUC | 95% interval | Beats chance? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laya outlook (P higher - P lower) | 0.497 | 0.427 to 0.560 | no |
| Laya 'buy now?' yes/no | 0.527 | 0.457 to 0.588 | no |
| Rules: regime gate passes | 0.474 | 0.417 to 0.538 | no |
| Information ceiling: logistic regression on the same numbers | 0.544 | 0.468 to 0.605 | no |

Rank correlation of the Laya score with the actual 10-day return: -0.014.
Probability accuracy (Brier, lower is better): Laya raw 0.3832, Laya calibrated on 2018-2022 0.2478, always guessing the historical up-rate 0.2477.

## Efficiency on this PC (cuda)

- Laya: model load 13.1s, ~19 ms per daily decision (3071 newly scored, 32 from cache), about 1.7 GB of weights on disk.
- Rules: ~0.003 ms per day of history for all indicators and gates.
- For one decision a day, both are instant in practice. Speed isn't the question; decision quality is.

## Verdict

SKIP Laya: it did not beat chance on unseen data and/or did not improve trading results.
- Fine-tuning outlook: even a model trained directly on these numbers can't rank unseen 10-day outcomes better than chance, so fine-tuning Laya on the same information is very unlikely to create an edge.
